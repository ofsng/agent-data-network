# Operations and legal guardrails
Only synthetic, nonpersonal data and disposable Devnet funds; no mainnet endpoints in active settings. Demo license is illustrative, not a commercial legal agreement. Hash is not ownership/consent/quality proof. Public receipts expose wallet relationships, price and hash; do not put private terms, prompts, personal or low-entropy real content on chain. No public keys-to-identity claims. No publicly available server-side signer; protected local demo. Audit log chain IDs, timestamps, errors, not secrets. If leakage: disable endpoints, rotate test keys, record incident; cannot delete chain history. If delivery unavailable: preserve receipt, restore exact bytes, retry; no automated refund claim. Bounty requester may refuse valid work; disclose; no arbitration. Post-event legal gates: actual rights/license, data processing, KVKK/GDPR where relevant, payment jurisdiction (Turkey crypto payment restrictions require qualified review), consumer/contract/refund/tax obligations, program audit and upgrade governance. No company/equity arrangement selected. No real sales until separately authorized. Solo developer/AI agent by default; if team, assign program, app, testing/demo owners explicitly. No physical market work.
